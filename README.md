One front page summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026:
35 stories, 13 layered illustrations, a numbers band of six verified
figures, and a world map whose numbered pins match the numbered index.

- Masthead is the date itself: **Saturday 21 November 2026**.
- Hero stories: Iran's strikes on US bases in Bahrain, Mexico's capture
  of "El Chapo Isidro", and the COP31 climate talks running past deadline
  in Antalya with no deal.
- Every story is from 21 November 2026 only — see the DATE CHECK footer.
- `draw.py` draws the page with Pillow (deterministic, no network);
  `main.ts` copies the shipped `entry.png` to the output directory.
